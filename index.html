Welcome to FieldReady Competency Study

Before using this evaluator, review and acknowledge the safeguards below. These controls are intended to protect students, evaluators, and official training records.

Program ownership
Training/evaluation use onlyUse this application to support standardized TCCC competency assessment for the nurse longitudinal study. It is not clinical decision support and is not an operational casualty or medical record.
Minimum necessary student dataUse only the student information required for an authorized training record. Do not enter SSNs, DoD ID numbers, patient identifiers, diagnoses, or unnecessary personal information.
No protected operational/medical dataDo not enter classified information, CUI, PHI, patient/casualty information, or other protected data unless this application and hosting environment are specifically approved for that data.
Protect exportsPDF, CSV, and JSON exports may contain names and official training results. Store and transmit them only through approved organizational systems and according to applicable records/privacy requirements.
Local-storage riskThis web/PWA build stores working records in this browser on this device. Clearing site data, private-browsing resets, browser/profile changes, or device loss can remove records. Export/backup completed classes promptly.
Verify current standardsDHA/JTS/DAF source references support the evaluator, but the current official standard and authorized course guidance remain controlling if a discrepancy is identified.

Acknowledgment is required once per app session. No acknowledgment is transmitted or centrally stored by this version.

FieldReady Competency Study

Browser-based, evaluator-focused competency assessment for the longitudinal nurse study. Includes the protected CMC Tactical Trauma Assessment plus the selected tourniquet, NPA, needle decompression, and blood-administration skills.
